More than half of web visits happen on phones. A site that looks great on your computer can be cramped and tiny on a phone, so check both.

## Preview at phone size

Your browser can pretend to be a phone:

1. With your page open in Chrome or Edge, press **F12** (Windows) or **Option-Command-I** (Mac) to open the developer tools.
2. Click the small phone-and-tablet icon at the top of the tools panel.
3. Choose a phone, such as iPhone, from the menu above the page.

You can also send the file to your phone, but the browser's phone view is quicker.

## Common fixes to ask for

- "On phone screens, stack the three cards in one column."
- "Make sure nothing scrolls sideways on a phone."
- "Make the body text at least 18 pixels and the line spacing comfortable."
- "Make links and buttons big enough to tap easily."

## Readable for everyone

Your readers may include people with low vision, color blindness, or shaky hands. A few requests cover most of it:

<div class="try">

**Try it now.**

> Review my web page for accessibility. Check color contrast, text size, alt text on images, heading order, and tap target size. List problems in plain English and give me the corrected code. [paste your HTML]
