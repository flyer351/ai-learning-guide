## A custom web address (optional)

An address like `smithfamilystories.com` is easier to share than `cheerful-otter-12345.netlify.app`. Domain names usually cost **$10 to $20 a year** from registrars such as Cloudflare, Porkbun, Namecheap or Amazon's Route 53.

1. Search for and buy your name at a registrar. Skip the add-ons: you don't need their website builder, email bundle or "SEO package." Do make sure **WHOIS privacy** (sometimes called domain privacy) is on, so your home address isn't listed in the public record for the domain. Most registrars include it free.
2. In Netlify or GitHub Pages settings, add your custom domain. They'll show you a couple of settings (called DNS records) to copy into your registrar's control panel.
3. Wait an hour or so. Turn on **HTTPS** (the padlock) when it's offered. It's free.

Your AI assistant can walk you through the exact screens: "I bought a domain at [registrar] and want to point it to my [GitHub Pages / Netlify] site. Walk me through it."

<div class="warn">

**Good to know.** Domain renewals can cost more than the first year. Turn on auto-renew so you don't lose the name, and note the renewal price before you buy.

</div>

## A privacy policy page

If you publish an app, Apple requires a privacy policy web address. If your site or app collects nothing, a short page saying so is enough:

<div class="try">

**Try it now.**

> Write a short, plain-English privacy policy page in HTML for my iPhone app "[name]". The app stores all data only on the user's device, collects no personal information, has no ads or analytics, and shares nothing with anyone. Include a contact email: [email].

</div>

## Keep it fresh

- Add something small every month or two. A new project, a story, a photo.
- Ask AI to proofread new pages before you publish.
- Check your links once a year: "Here's my page. List every link so I can check they still work."
