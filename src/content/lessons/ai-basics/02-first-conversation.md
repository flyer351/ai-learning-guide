An AI assistant is a website or app you type to in everyday language, and it types back. There's no special wording to learn. If you can write an email, you can do this.

## Which assistant?

The big four all have good free versions. Pick one to start. You can try the others later.

<div class="table">

| Assistant | Web address |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT, by OpenAI | [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) |
| Claude, by Anthropic | [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) |
| Gemini, by Google | [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) |

</div>

Each also has a free app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Here's what you'll do

1. Tap or click the web address above, or type it into your browser, such as Safari, Chrome or Edge.
2. Sign up with your email address, or with your Google, Apple or Microsoft account. No credit card is needed.
3. Find the message box. On a phone it's at the bottom of the screen; on a computer it may start in the middle. It usually says something like "Ask anything" or "How can I help?"
4. Type your question and press Enter (or tap the arrow button).
5. Read the reply, then ask a follow-up, just as you would with a person.

<div class="try">

**Try it now.** Paste this in as your first message, changing the details to fit you:

> I spent 30 years in manufacturing and I'm new to AI. Explain what you can help me with, in plain English, in five short bullet points.

</div>

## Why that message works

It tells the assistant three things: **who you are**, **what you want**, and **how you'd like the answer**. Those three ingredients improve almost any reply. You'll practice them in the next lesson.

## Keep the conversation going

The real power is in the follow-up. Try replies like:

- "Tell me more about number three."
- "Give me an example from everyday life."
- "That's too technical. Explain it like you would to a neighbor."

<div class="warn">

**Good to know.** AI can sound sure of itself and still be wrong. Check anything about health, money or law with a trusted source.

</div>
