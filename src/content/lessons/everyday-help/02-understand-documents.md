Insurance explanations of benefits, Medicare notices, contracts, user manuals, legal notices: AI is excellent at translating them into plain English.

## How to give it a document

- **Copy and paste** the text into the message box.
- **Upload a file.** Look for a paperclip or **+** button next to the message box. PDFs and Word files work.
- **Take a photo.** In the phone app, tap the camera or **+** button and photograph the page. The AI can read it.

Remember lesson 5 of AI basics: cover or crop out account numbers, Social Security numbers and other identifiers first.

<div class="try">

**Try it now.** Find a confusing letter or statement in your mail. Upload it, or paste the text, with this message:

> Explain this in plain English. What is it, do I need to do anything, and by when? List anything that looks unusual or worth asking about.

</div>

## Questions that work well

- "What does this term mean: 'coinsurance after deductible'?"
- "Is anything in this contract unusual compared to a typical contract of this kind?"
- "What questions should I ask before signing?"
- "Make me a checklist of what this letter asks me to do."

## Contracts deserve a second look

AI can point out things to ask about, such as cancellation windows, automatic renewals, arbitration clauses and fees. That's very valuable. But it isn't a lawyer, and it can miss things. For anything costly or long-term, use it to prepare your questions, then ask a professional.

<div class="takeaway">

**The takeaway.** AI turns "I'll deal with this later" paperwork into a five-minute task. Use it to understand, then decide for yourself.

</div>
