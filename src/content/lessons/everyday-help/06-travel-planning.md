Travel planning is a perfect AI task: lots of options, lots of details, and plenty of room for your preferences.

## Three stages of a trip

Most trips come together in the same order: decide where to go, find good places to eat, and work out how to get there. AI helps with each stage.

<div class="try">

**Try it now.** Start with stage 1:

> We live in Atlanta and want a relaxed spring trip within a day's drive. We love gardens, history and good seafood, and one of us has a bad knee. Suggest three places and say in one sentence why each fits us.

</div>

Then keep going in the same chat:

- **Stage 2:** "We picked Charleston and we're staying downtown. Suggest five seafood restaurants within a short, flat walk of the historic district, from casual to special-occasion."
- **Stage 3:** "We're driving from Atlanta. Suggest a halfway stop with a clean restroom, a good lunch and an easy short walk to stretch our legs."

When it's time to drive, open the address in **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps** for live directions and traffic. AI can suggest a route, but it can't see today's roadwork or closures.

## Build an itinerary

The more you tell it about how you like to travel, the better the plan.

<div class="try">

**Try it now.**

> Plan a relaxed 5-day trip to Charleston, South Carolina, in April for two people in their late 60s. We like history, good seafood and gardens. We don't want more than two main activities a day, and one of us has a bad knee, so limit long walks. Include a rough daily budget.

</div>

Then refine: "Swap day 3 for something rainy-day friendly." "Add a nice dinner spot for our anniversary."

## Other handy uses

- **Packing lists:** "Packing list for a 10-day Alaska cruise in June, carry-on only."
- **Road trips:** "Driving from Kansas City to Nashville. Suggest a halfway stop with a good motel and an interesting sight."
- **Day outings:** "Ideas for a day trip within two hours of Columbus, Ohio, for grandkids aged 8 and 11."
- **Phrasebooks:** "Teach me 15 useful Italian phrases for restaurants, with pronunciation."

## Check before you book

Hours, prices, and even whether a place is still open can be out of date. Confirm on the official site before you book, and book through the airline, hotel or a well-known travel site, never through a link an AI or a stranger made up.
