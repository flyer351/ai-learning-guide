Need a new dishwasher? Curious about solar panels? Reading up on a town before a move? AI can save hours of clicking.

## Learning a topic fast

Start broad, then narrow down:

1. "Give me a beginner's overview of heat pumps for a home in a cold climate."
2. "What are the main questions to ask an installer?"
3. "What are common complaints people have after installing one?"

## Comparing products

<div class="try">

**Try it now.** Adapt this to something you're actually shopping for:

> I need a new dishwasher under $900. Quiet operation and reliability matter most; I don't care about Wi-Fi features. What features should I look for, what should I avoid, and which brands are generally considered reliable? Search the web for current information and include your sources.

</div>

Notice the last sentence. Asking it to **search the web** and **include sources** gets you current information and links you can check. Most assistants can search now, but sometimes only when asked.

## Avoiding the sales spin

- "What are the downsides?" Every product has some.
- "What do people regret about buying this?"
- "Is the extended warranty usually worth it for this kind of product?"

## Local and current information

Store hours, prices and availability change fast. Use AI to narrow down your options, then confirm on the store's own website or by phone.

<div class="takeaway">

**The takeaway.** Use AI as a knowledgeable friend who has read the reviews. Make the final call with current information from the source.

</div>
